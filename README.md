# BHOOMI-Net 2.0
### Autonomous Multi-Hazard Early Warning & AI Afforestation Engine
**Digital Twin & Simulation of an Intelligent Environmental Monitoring Network**

---

## 🌍 Overview

**BHOOMI-Net 2.0** is an interactive, futuristic environmental command center and digital twin. It monitors a disaster-prone landscape using a simulated edge sensor network (Drishti-Cam, Bhoomi-Probe soil sensors, NDVI, LiDAR, GPS, and LoRa mesh), estimates multi-hazard risk probabilities, simulates disaster progression step-by-step, and autonomously closes the mitigation loop by prescribing an ecological afforestation plan with GPS-accurate planting points and native species selection.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SIMULATION NOTICE & DIGITAL TWIN DISCLAIMER**:
> This software is an educational simulation and digital twin prototype. All sensor readings, hazard curves, lead times, and tree-count calculations are **SIMULATED DATA** generated by empirical mathematical models. BHOOMI AI estimates conditional hazard vulnerability based on sensor indicators and does not claim to guarantee the exact timing of real-world disasters.

- **Restoration Targets**: Canopy 24.2% ➔ 68.5%, Bare soil 75.8% ➔ 31.5%, ~4,850 estimated saplings.
- **3 Ecological Zones**:
  1. **Zone 1 — Waterfront (Riparian Buffer)**:
     - Area: 2.2 ha (22%) | Target Canopy: 72% | Spacing: 3.5 m | Saplings: ~1,450
     - Species: *Terminalia arjuna* (Arjuna), *Bambusa bambos* (Bamboo), Native Riparian Reeds
     - Purpose: Riverbank tensile stabilization, water velocity dissipation, flood attenuation.
  2. **Zone 2 — Hillside (Slope Bioengineering)**:
     - Area: 5.2 ha (52%) | Target Canopy: 68% | Spacing: 4.5 m | Saplings: ~2,400
     - Species: Deep-Root Neem (*Azadirachta indica*), Jamun (*Syzygium cumini*), Vetiver grass (*Chrysopogon zizanioides*)

  3. **Zone 3 — Ridge / Forest Fringe (Fire Resilience)**:
     - Area: 2.6 ha (26%) | Target Canopy: 62% | Spacing: 5.5 m | Saplings: ~1,000
     - Species: Mahua (*Madhuca longifolia*), Sal (*Shorea robusta*), Native Broadleaf (*Ficus benghalensis*)
     - Purpose: Living green firebreak, canopy moisture retention, radiant heat suppression.
- **Tree Placement Exclusion Rules**:
  - 🟢 Green crosshair: Optimal planting site.
  - 🟡 Yellow crosshair: Moderate suitability (requires contour trenching or soil conditioning).
  - 🔴 Red marker: Unsuitable / Exclusion zone (active water channel core, road corridor, sheer vertical cliff > 45°, existing mature canopy).

## 🛠️ Zero External Dependencies Required

The application runs directly in any modern browser without npm, node_modules, webpack, or backend servers. It loads lightweight CDN assets for Three.js, OrbitControls, Chart.js, and Leaflet, and leverages native browser APIs (Web Audio API and Web Speech API) for zero-latency audio and voice synthesis.
