🌧️ Flash Flood Risk Model

Risk = 0.30·Rain + 0.25·SoilM + 0.20·BareSoil + 0.15·Slope + 0.10·RunoffV
Weights rainfall accumulation and soil saturation against exposed bare topsoil runoff speed.

🔥 Wildfire Propagation Model

Risk = 0.25·Temp + 0.25·(100-Humidity) + 0.20·(1-NDVI) + 0.15·Wind + 0.15·Fuel
Synthesizes ambient thermal elevation, desiccated foliage NDVI, and wind vector corridors.

⛰️ Landslide Slip Risk Model

Risk = 0.30·Slope + 0.25·SoilM + 0.20·LiDAR Slip + 0.15·Rain + 0.10·Erosion
Calculates slope shear instability against hydrostatic pore water pressure and lack of anchoring roots.
